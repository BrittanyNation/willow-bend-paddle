How a rental day works

  1. 1. Book online. Pick a craft, a day, and a launch time. You get a booking reference right away — no account, no phone call.
  2. 2. Show up at the dock. We fit your life jacket, walk the put-in, and point out the best lunch rocks on the bend.
  3. 3. Paddle and return. Float back to the dock by your return time. Late returns are $10 per half hour, stated here so there are no surprises.
